From Idea to Google Wallet in 3 Steps

Designed for developers, issuers, and power users testing or issuing Google Wallet passes from Android.

1

Design & Import

Choose any of the 7 pass types, customize colors and fields, or import an existing barcode/QR straight from an image.

2

Sign On-Device

Enter your Issuer ID and import your Google Cloud Service Account JSON in Settings—stored persistently on-device for RS256 JWT signing.

3

Save, Link, or Share

Tap Add to Google Wallet via the native PayClient SDK, open the pay.google.com/gp/v/save/ URL, scan the Save QR, or copy/share the JSON & JWT.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 • Developer / Operator: Niks Ļeoņenko

This Privacy Policy describes how NL Pass Builder (package com.example.walletpassgenerator), developed and operated by Niks Ļeoņenko, handles user data when you use our Android application and website.

1. Data We Process & Local-First Storage

NL Pass Builder is designed with an on-device, local-first architecture. We do not operate a central backend server to collect or store your personal passes.

  • Pass Content & Barcodes: Any pass fields, text modules, links, or barcode values you enter or import from an image are processed locally on your device.
  • Images Selected for Barcode Import: When you import a barcode or QR code from an image, the image is decoded locally on-device using ZXing and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Issuer & Service Account Credentials: Optional Google Wallet Issuer IDs and Google Cloud Service Account JSON keys you provide are stored locally on your device in private app storage (SharedPreferences) solely to sign savetowallet JWTs on-device.

2. Third-Party Services (Google Wallet API)

When you tap Add to Google Wallet or open a generated https://pay.google.com/gp/v/save/... link, the pass payload or signed JWT you created is transmitted directly to Google LLC via the Google Wallet Android SDK (PayClient) or Google Wallet web flow in order to save the pass to your Google Wallet account. Use of Google Wallet is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

3. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, pass contents, or signing credentials to any third parties.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All configuration data and signing keys remain on your device only until you clear them (via Clear Key in Settings), clear app storage, or uninstall NL Pass Builder. If you have any questions or requests regarding data deletion or privacy, contact us using the details below.
